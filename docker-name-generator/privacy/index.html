Privacy policy

Docker Name Generator is a small static website. It does not require an account.

Analytics

This tool is part of kyrylo.org and uses its shared Owlytics analytics to understand aggregate traffic. Each visit is recorded once under kyrylo.org, with the page path identifying this tool. It does not load a separate Docker Name Generator analytics tracker.

Tool events

The generator sends tool actions to its existing Feeds event feed, such as when names are generated or copied. Feeds is used only on the generator page, and it does not record pageviews. These events can include generated names, selected separator, requested and generated counts, copy row position, generated Docker command text, page path, referrer host, browser language, viewport size, color scheme, time zone, and related tool context. Do not put private information into generated names or nearby tooling.

Cookies and advertising

The site does not currently show ads. If advertising such as Google AdSense is enabled later, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits.

Visitors can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. They can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

External links

This site links to other websites, including Docker, GitHub, Telebugs, and Kyrylo's other projects. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent through the links on the contact page.
